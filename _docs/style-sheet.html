Estovam
Tech

Estovam Tech builds small autonomous robots from off-the-shelf parts. The site reads like the robot's own instrument panel: a dark ground, a two-circle logo, giant condensed type, and small mono readouts that report what the machine is doing.

Brand rules

Voice//Color//Type//Layout

Voice

  • Two voices. The company speaks in short plain sentences: "Built from readily available parts, so the robot is easier to source, build, repair, and upgrade." The machine speaks in hud labels: "// ROBOTICS ENGINEERING", "AZ 132°", "LIVE MODEL".
  • HUD labels are uppercase facts separated by //. Never a full sentence in mono, never jokes or fake alarms.
  • Name real parts: "Raspberry Pi 5", "ReSpeaker microphone board", "Arducam IMX519 camera".
  • Write headings in sentence case in the source; the display styles uppercase them.
  • The tagline is Precise. Efficient. Autonomous. Use it once, in the hero. The description line is "Developing intelligent robotic systems that improve automation through precision, efficiency, and autonomy."
  • Actions end in a plus: "Contact us +", "Hide shell +", "Inside +".
  • No emoji, no exclamation marks, no superlatives.

Color

  • Dark is the default: ink on ground, which is the logo's #111111. The light theme mirrors the black-on-white logo.
  • Supporting copy is mute. faint is for decoration only (coordinates, //), never information.
  • Separate list rows with line hairlines; open sections and draw frames with line-strong.
  • Show the robot on stage in both themes, with stage-ink readouts.
  • signal is the one color and it stays small: the live dot, the clock dot, the selected row's index, the hover underline on the contact link. Never fill a section, a headline or a button with it.
  • dot draws the dot-matrix texture behind the hero and the viewer.

Type

  • Archivo at width 62%, weight 800, uppercase, for everything big: display-cta, display-xl, display-l, heading, button. The es-display class sets the width and case.
  • Contact us is the loudest thing on the site. display-cta runs nearly edge to edge at the bottom of every page.
  • Archivo regular for lead and body; row for names in tables.
  • Martian Mono at width 75% for hud, readout and index. Readouts use tabular figures so numbers don't jitter.

Shape and layout

  • Everything is square: radius-0. The only round things are the logo and radius-full status dots.
  • Media and the 3D viewer sit in a HUD frame: stage fill, dot texture, 12px corner brackets in line-strong, readouts pinned to the corners.
  • 12-column grid inside layout-max with a space-5 gutter. Index tables run 1 / 5 / 5 / 1: number, name, description, count.
  • A fixed bottom bar (space-12 tall, raise) carries the nav, the local clock and the contact button. The top of the page carries only the logo and one HUD line.
  • Sections open with a line-strong rule and space-16 padding. Everything stacks under layout-break.

Imagery and motion

  • Imagery is see-through CAD renders and the live 3D model on stage. No stock photos, no people.
  • The viewer auto-rotates slowly until touched and reports its camera angle live (AZ, EL, zoom). "Hide shell +" removes the frosted shell; selecting a row in the parts index isolates that group. No rotation under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • The clock ticks every second and its signal dot blinks once a second; both stop blinking under reduced motion.
  • Hover inverts (ink and ground swap) or underlines. Nothing slides or bounces.
  • Focus is a 2px signal outline offset 3px.

Logo

  • Use the files in Logos. On dark grounds use lockup-white.png or mark-white.png; on light grounds lockup-black.png or mark-black.png. The two ...-on-... squares are for avatars (LinkedIn, GitHub).
  • The header uses the lockup at 28px tall. Leave clear space of one big-circle height around it.
  • Never recolor the mark in signal, outline it, rotate it or put it on a render.

Icons

  • There is no icon set. Use words with a plus for actions and a signal dot plus a word for status.

Logo

The two-circle logo, as the lockup or the mark alone, swapping white and black files with the theme.

  • Provide: both files (lockup-white.png + lockup-black.png, or mark-white.png + mark-black.png) inside es-logo, with classes for-dark and for-light; the stylesheet shows the right one.
  • The header uses the lockup at 28px tall; the mark alone is for small spaces (favicon, avatar, the bottom bar on phones).
  • Never recolor, outline, rotate or place it on a render.

Lockup//Mark//Header size 28px

Header

The top of every page: the logo lockup and one HUD line, over a hairline.

  • Provide: the HUD line (category and tagline, or the page name). Navigation lives in the BottomBar, not here.
  • Not sticky. On phones the HUD line wraps under the logo.

BottomBar

A fixed bar along the bottom of the screen: section links, the visitor's local time, and the contact button.

  • Provide: up to four section links (anchors), and the clock (update every second from the visitor's own clock; label with their time zone abbreviation).
  • Add es-bar--fixed on the live site and pad the page bottom by 48px so it never covers content.
  • Under 760px the clock hides; links and the button stay.

Button

Square, condensed, uppercase buttons that end in a plus.

  • Provide: a label naming what happens ("Contact us", "Hide shell"); the plus is added by the class.
  • es-btn is solid ink and inverts on hover; es-btn--line is the secondary; es-btn--sm fits inside frames. es-link is the text-only nav link.
  • Always radius-0, 48px tall (the module). One solid button per view.

Tag

The machine's voice: status tags, live readouts and HUD label lines.

  • es-tag is a signal dot plus one or two words of state. Add es-tag--blink only for something live right now.
  • es-readout is a boxed number with its unit (AZ, EL, zoom, time). Solid on ground, outlined inside a frame.
  • es-hud is a line of uppercase facts split by <span class="sep">//</span>; wrap the one fact that matters in <b>.
  • Never a dot without a word. Never a sentence in mono.

Viewer

The 3D turnaround of the robot inside a HUD frame. The preview shows a still render where the WebGL canvas goes.

  • Provide: the three.js canvas (OrbitControls), the "Hide shell" toggle, and the camera readouts.
  • Readouts update live from the camera: azimuth and elevation in whole degrees, zoom to one decimal.
  • Auto-rotate slowly until the first touch; never under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Pair it with PartsIndex: selecting a row isolates that group in the model.
  • If WebGL fails, the frame shows "The 3D model could not load in this browser." in stage-ink.

PartsIndex

A ruled, numbered index of part groups that drives the Viewer.

  • Provide: rows of number, part group, plain description and piece count. Numbers are two digits; counts are right-aligned tabular mono.
  • Selecting a row inverts it (ink fill) and turns its number signal; selecting again clears it. Each row needs a real button or aria-selected for keyboard users.
  • The description column hides under 760px.
